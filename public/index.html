🍊 How points work

Session Score
Sess = (avg point diff + 10) × session rating
Your average points difference per game, lifted by +10 so everyone starts positive, then scaled by how strong the session was.
Win Bonus
Bonus = (win% ÷ 50) × session rating
Win every game → +2× session rating bonus. Win half → +1× session rating bonus.
🍊 Avg (your score)
Score = average of (Sess + Bonus) across all sessions
Your league position is your average session score across every approved session you've played. More sessions = more reliable ranking. Shown as provisional until 5 sessions.
Session Rating
Each session has a rating based on who played — the average of all player ratings. Stronger sessions are worth more points, so beating a strong group counts for more than an easy one.

How it works

📱 Accelerometer — measures peak G-force during your swing and converts it to estimated racket head speed using biomechanical modelling of a badminton smash motion.

🎤 Audio — detects the sharp impact transient of the shuttle hit and uses timing to refine the estimate.

⚠️ Note: This is an estimate, not a radar gun. Accuracy depends on how you hold the phone. Results are best used for comparing your own smashes — not comparing with others.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Shuttler is a badminton session scoring and league management app. We take your privacy seriously.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address when you sign in
  • Game scores and session results you enter
  • Your profile photo if you upload one
  • RSVP responses to scheduled sessions

How we use it

  • To display your scores, standings, and stats
  • To show your name to other league members
  • To send you relevant league notifications
  • We never sell your data to third parties

Data storage

Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Firestore). You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings.

Add to home screen

Installing makes a tap-to-open icon on your device and runs it full-screen, like a normal app. It still updates itself when you open it online.

Make sure you're in Safari — adding to the home screen doesn't work from Chrome or an in-app browser on iPhone/iPad.

1Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up (bottom of the screen on iPhone, top on iPad).
2Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
3Tap Add — the Shuttler icon appears on your home screen like any other app.

Works from Chrome on Android.

1Tap the ⋮ menu button in the top-right corner.
2Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
3Confirm — the Shuttler icon appears on your home screen like any other app.

Works from Chrome or Edge on Windows, Mac, or Chromebook.

1Look for the install icon (a little monitor with a down arrow) at the right of the address bar.
2Click it and choose Install. If you don't see the icon, open the ⋮ menu and look for Install Shuttler… instead.
3Shuttler opens in its own window and gets an icon in your taskbar/dock.
